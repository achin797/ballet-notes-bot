Grouping principle: file each correction under the step or combo it belongs to. Three exceptions:

* A correction about a position or shape (arabesque, attitude, passé, développé, etc.) that isn't tied to one exercise gets its own heading — don't force it under whatever step it appeared in.
* A correction that's a broad principle across multiple steps (turnout, full foot point, spiral, hip/pelvis lock, upright chest — examples only, others will come up) goes in General/Cross-cutting, not repeated under each step.
* If I can't tell where something belongs and it's a ballet universal (stomach in, point foot), treat it as General. If I explicitly wrote it under a step, keep it there as that step's observation.
