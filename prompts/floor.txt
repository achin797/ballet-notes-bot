6. **What clicked** — discoveries and felt-senses that landed this session, in my exact imagery. This is what I'm trying to re-access next time. Omit the section entirely if there are none.
7. **Try next time** — untried ideas, experiments ("try...", "maybe placebo, check next time"), biases to unlearn, or things I said to keep an eye on. Omit entirely if there are none.
