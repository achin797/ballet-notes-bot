PLACEHOLDER — the real condensing prompt for a FLOOR BARRE session goes here.

Everything below the marker line is sent to the model verbatim, with {RAW_NOTES}
replaced by the notes as they arrived on Telegram (newline-joined, in order).

The model's reply becomes the Notion page body as-is, so the prompt must specify
markdown output with no preamble and no sign-off — the first characters of the
reply are the first characters of the page.

Kept separate from prompts/class.txt on purpose, so a floor barre session can ask
different things of the notes than a class does. If the two prompts end up saying
the same thing, that is fine — nothing is lost by having split them.
