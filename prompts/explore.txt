Structure my raw movement exploration notes into clear, concise notes.

Focus only on what I observed, experienced, noticed, or did.

Do not draw conclusions, diagnose patterns, create theories, or over-interpret.

Preserve uncertainty. If I say “maybe,” “I think,” or “I don’t know,” keep that uncertainty.

Useful sections:

Awareness

* What I noticed in my body, mind, energy, balance, tension, etc.

Intention

* What I was trying to bring attention to or explore.

Movement

* Movements, qualities, pathways, levels, rhythms, or actions that actually happened.

What Felt Alive

* Moments that felt interesting, freeing, smooth, difficult, surprising, etc.

What Felt Stuck

* Moments of blockage, hesitation, repetition, or difficulty.

Observations

* Anything notable from the session that is worth remembering.

Questions / Things to Explore

* Only questions or ideas I explicitly mentioned in my raw notes. Do not invent new ones.

Keep notes concise. Do not turn single-session observations into “patterns” or conclusions. Do not add anything that isn’t present in my raw notes.
